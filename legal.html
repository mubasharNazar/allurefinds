Privacy Policy

AllureFinds respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or place an order, you share details such as your name, phone number, email, city and order notes. These are sent to us through WhatsApp or email at your initiative.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, mock-ups and invoices
  • To produce and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order or enquiry

What stays on your device

Your bag and wishlist are stored in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to send an order to us.

Sharing

We never sell your data. We only share delivery details with courier partners to fulfil your order.

Questions? Email hello@allurefinds.com.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by courier delays or events beyond our control.
