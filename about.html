Our story

Small details, lasting impressions

AllureFinds began with a simple belief: a gift should feel personal, even when it's one of a thousand.

From a tiny print studio to a full-service gifting house

What started as a small print counter in Islamabad has grown into a team of designers, artisans and event producers serving brands across Pakistan.

We obsess over the things most people never notice — the weight of a pen, the depth of a deboss, the way a ribbon sits on a box. Because those are the details your clients and colleagues remember.

FAQ

Good to know

What is the minimum order quantity?

Most items can be ordered as a single piece. Bulk tiers start at 25 pieces, and prices drop further at 100 and 500+.

Will I see a design before production?

Yes — we share a free digital mock-up for every order and only start production once you approve it.

How long does production take?

Typically 5–7 working days after approval. Rush orders are possible; tell us your deadline when you enquire.

Do you deliver outside Islamabad?

We deliver countrywide through trusted courier partners, and can ship to multiple addresses for distributed teams.

How do I pay?

We confirm your order on WhatsApp and share bank transfer details. Corporate clients can request an invoice with advance + balance terms.
